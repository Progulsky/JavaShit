# Expression

**Related notes:** [[Statement]], [[Literal]], [[Variable]], [[Operator]]

---

An **expression** is a programming construct that evaluates to a **single value**. It commonly utilizes **operators** to perform operations on **variables** or **literals**. These operations include:

- Arithmetic and logical operations.
    
- Concatenation of strings.
    
- Assignment of value.
    
- Method calls.

---

### Expression Examples

|**Type**|**Example**|
|---|---|
|**Arithmetic**|`5 + 5`|
|**Variable-based**|`var1 * var2`|
|**String Concatenation**|`"Hello" + " " + "Java"`|
|**Method Call**|`Math.sqrt(16)`|
